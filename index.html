About The Omega Group

The Omega Group is a premium interior design and turnkey execution studio, crafting refined homes and commercial spaces for clients who value thoughtful design as much as flawless delivery. For over 10 years, we have partnered with luxury homeowners, villa and apartment owners, architects, and businesses to turn ambitious ideas into interiors that feel personal, effortless, and enduring.

Every project begins with a conversation, not a template. Our design team studies how you live and work, then carries that understanding through concept, material selection, and on-site execution — managing every stage under one accountable roof, so you never have to coordinate multiple vendors.
