Home Interiors in Gurgaon, Planned Around Your Type of Home

A new 3 BHK in a high-rise, an independent builder floor and a multi-level villa each need a different plan. The site rules are different, so is the amount of civil work, and so is where the budget should go. Here's how we approach each one in Gurgaon.

High-Rise Apartments

Many newer towers along Dwarka Expressway, New Gurgaon and Sohna Road come with society rules on working hours, lift use for material and work permissions. We plan the site schedule around these from day one.

Because walls and service shafts are fixed, the design work goes into smart layouts and storage planned into every room, whether it's a 2, 3 or 4 BHK. Our 4 BHK interior design guide shows how this works in a larger flat.

Independent Builder Floors

Builder floors in areas like DLF and Sushant Lok usually give you more freedom to change the layout. They also tend to need more groundwork, so flooring, electrical and plumbing are planned before any furniture is designed.

Where the structure allows, rooms can be reworked one by one, with electrical and plumbing settled before any carpentry starts. Read our builder floor interior design guide for more detail.

Villas and Large Homes

In a multi-level home, the challenge is keeping one design language across every floor, from the entrance and staircase to the bedrooms. Larger homes also leave more room for custom-made furniture, like the work we did at M3M Woodshire.

We keep one material and colour palette running through the whole house, size bespoke furniture to each room and plan the lighting floor by floor, so the home feels connected rather than pieced together.

What's Included in Turnkey Home Interiors

In Gurgaon, "interior design" can mean a full turnkey project, a modular-only package or a design-only consultation. Two quotes for the same home can look very different for that reason alone. This is what our turnkey scope covers, so you can compare quotes like for like.

01

Design & Planning

We start with space planning and furniture layouts, then show each room in 3D so you can see it before anything is built. Once you've chosen materials and finishes, we prepare detailed working drawings for the site and workshop teams.

02

Ceilings, Lighting & Finishes

This covers the false ceiling (in POP or gypsum), a lighting plan with the electrical points to support it, and painting and wall finishes throughout the home.

03

Kitchen, Wardrobes & Furniture

Your modular kitchen cabinets, shutters and hardware are included, along with wardrobes, TV, shoe and crockery units, and bespoke furniture made to fit. Not sure which pieces to build custom? See modular vs custom furniture.

04

Execution & Handover

We source the materials, our own team supervises the site, and we handle installation through to the final handover, so there's one point of contact from start to finish.

Choosing Materials That Suit Gurgaon's Climate

Gurgaon has hot, dusty summers and a humid monsoon, and the water in many areas is hard. Finishes that look good on handover day also need to stand up to these conditions for years. These are the choices we talk through with every homeowner.

Summer Heat and Dust

Layered curtains or blinds help with strong west-facing sun. Matte, easy-to-wipe surfaces and closed storage instead of large open shelves keep dust in check, and we plan AC points and ducting into the ceiling design from the start.

Monsoon Humidity

In kitchens and bathroom vanities we recommend boiling-water-resistant (BWP) plywood, with edge banding on every exposed edge to keep moisture out. Good ventilation for kitchens, bathrooms and wardrobes is planned in as well.

Hard Water

Non-porous counters such as quartz work well near sinks, and we pick fittings and finishes that are easy to descale. If you want a water softener, we leave space for it at the plumbing stage.
