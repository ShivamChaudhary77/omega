This policy explains what personal information The Omega Group collects through theomegagroup.in, why we collect it, how it's stored, and the choices you have. It covers our contact form, our use of Google Analytics, and the third-party services (Google Maps, WhatsApp) embedded on this site.

Last updated: August 2026. This policy is a plain-language summary of our data practices — it is not a substitute for legal advice, and we recommend having it reviewed against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023, and any other applicable law before relying on it for formal compliance purposes.

Who We Are

The Omega Group is a luxury interior design and turnkey execution studio based at Plot S4 & S5, Main Dwarka Expressway, Near Daulatabad Chowk, opp. Pillar 109, Gurugram, Haryana 122006, India. For any privacy-related question, contact us at thegroupomega@gmail.com or +91 981 1001 900.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: what you actively submit, and what's automatically recorded when you visit.

Information You Submit

When you fill out our contact form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • City
  • Details about your project that you choose to share

This is the minimum we need to follow up on your enquiry — we don't ask for anything beyond it on this form.

Information Collected Automatically

When you submit the contact form, our server also records the page you submitted it from, the page you arrived from (if any), your IP address, and your browser's user-agent string. This is standard practice for spam prevention and troubleshooting, not for tracking you individually.

Separately, while you browse the site, Google Analytics (GA4) collects standard analytics data — pages viewed, approximate location (city/country level, from IP), device/browser type, and interactions like clicking a "Call" or "WhatsApp" button or booking a consultation. See "Third-Party Services" below for how to opt out.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To understand which pages and content are useful to visitors, so we can improve the site
  • To detect and prevent spam submissions

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your contact details for marketing communications you haven't asked for.

How We Store Your Information

Contact form submissions are stored in a secured MySQL database on our hosting provider's servers, accessible only to authorized Omega Group staff. We don't publish, share, or export this data to any third party beyond what's described in this policy.

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site usage. Google may set cookies and process data according to its own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google Maps

Our Contact page and footer embed a Google Maps location. Loading this embed may allow Google to set cookies, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

WhatsApp

Our WhatsApp contact links open a chat via WhatsApp's own platform. Any conversation you have with us there is subject to WhatsApp's (Meta's) privacy policy, not this one.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics and Google Maps, as described above. We don't currently use any first-party tracking cookies of our own.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry and maintain business records, after which they may be archived or deleted. If you'd like your submission removed sooner, email us and we'll action it.

Your Rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it — email thegroupomega@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This site and our services are intended for adults seeking interior design services. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices or applicable law change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to thegroupomega@gmail.com or +91 981 1001 900.
