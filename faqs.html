No fixed minimum — we take on everything from a single modular kitchen or bespoke furniture piece to a full villa. Full-home turnkey interior design starts at ₹15 Lakhs+, scaling with size and customization.

Payments are milestone-based, tied to project stages (design sign-off, material procurement, execution phases, and handover) rather than one lump sum. Your exact payment schedule is confirmed in writing before work begins, so there are no surprises.
