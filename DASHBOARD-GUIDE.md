# Website dashboard: how to use it

The dashboard lets you change the website without any technical knowledge. Every change goes live the moment you press **Save** (unless the page is a draft).

**Address:** `https://theomegagroup.in/admin/` (available once the new site is switched on).

## Logging in
- There is one password for the dashboard. Keep it in a password manager and don't share it by email or chat.
- After 5 wrong passwords, logins from your connection are blocked for 15 minutes.
- You are logged out automatically after 2 hours without activity. Use **Log out** on shared computers.
- To change the password, go to **Settings → Change the dashboard password**.

## Finding a page
**Pages** lists every page on the site: blog posts, location pages, projects and the main pages. Use the search box or the type filter, then click a title or **Edit**.

## Editing a page
1. Change what you need. Fields marked <span style="color:#dc3545">*</span> are required.
2. The **Article** and **Text** boxes work like a word processor:
   - **Formatting:** bold, lists, links, tables and images.
   - **Headings:** use "Heading 2" for main sections and "Heading 3" for sub-points. Search engines rely on them.
3. **Designed sections** (home, about, services, contact and landing pages) open in **Edit text**. You see each piece of wording in its own box: change the words and the design stays exactly as it is. Avoid **Edit HTML (advanced)** unless you know HTML.
4. Use the arrows on a section to move it up or down, the bin to remove it, and **Add section** to add one.
5. Press **Save**. A green message confirms the change is live.

**Search engines (SEO)** on every page:
- **SEO title:** the blue link in Google. Aim for 50–60 characters.
- **Meta description:** the text under the link. Aim for 150–160 characters.

The counters under each box tell you when you're over.

## Adding a blog post
1. Click **New → Blog post**.
2. Fill in the title, category, featured image (with a short description of the photo), summary, article and SEO fields.
3. Leave **Status** on **Draft** while you work. Click **Preview saved version** to see it exactly as visitors will.
4. When it's ready, set **Status** to **Published** and **Save**. The post appears automatically on the blog page, on the home page ("From The Blog"), and in the sitemap that Google reads.

The post's address is made from its category and title, e.g. `/kitchen-design/your-title/`. Avoid changing the URL of a published page: old links and Google rankings point to it.

## SEO check
The **SEO check** box on every page (beside the editor, or above it on small screens) shows:
- **A Google preview:** how the page may look in search results. It updates as you type.
- **A checklist:** **!** marks something to fix, **i** marks a tip, and a tick means the check passed. It covers:
  - titles or descriptions that are too long or short
  - two pages with the same title
  - two posts aiming at the same search phrase
  - missing image descriptions
  - **broken links or images**
- **When it updates:** the checklist refreshes when you open or save the page.

## Changing a page's address, or deleting a page
- **Changing an address:** if you change the URL of a published page, the old address automatically sends visitors (and Google) to the new one. Old links keep working and rankings carry over.
- **Deleting a page:** when you move a published page to the trash, you choose where its visitors should go. The suggestion (for example the blog page for a deleted post) is usually right.
- **Redirects** in the top menu lists these automatic redirects. You can also add one by hand there (old address → new address), or remove one.

## Images
- **Choose…** next to an image field opens the picture library. Search by file name, or click **Upload new**.
- JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 5 MB are accepted. Large photos are resized and optimised automatically.
- **Images** in the top menu lists your uploads. Copy an address from there, or delete an image you no longer need. Deleting an image that a page still uses leaves a broken picture on that page.

## Mistakes and undo
- **Earlier versions:** every save keeps the previous version (the last 30). Open the page, click **Earlier versions**, then **Restore this version**.
- **Trash:** **Move to trash** takes a page off the website. Restore it from **Trash** at any time (any redirect from its address is removed automatically). **Delete for good** cannot be undone.
- **Home page:** it can't be deleted.

## Settings
**Settings** holds the details used across the whole site. Change them once and every page updates:
- phone, email, address and social links
- key figures (years of experience, projects delivered and so on)
- menu and footer links
- default wording of the consultation box
- tracking codes

## Please don't
- **Don't invent facts.** Never make up reviews, statistics, project sizes or budgets: only use real figures.
- **Don't target the same search phrase twice.** Before writing a new post, check that no other page already targets its main search phrase; ask your web team if unsure.
