Our Story

The Omega Group was founded on a simple belief: a home or workplace should feel as considered as it looks. What began as a small design practice has grown into a full turnkey interior studio, trusted by luxury homeowners, villa and apartment owners, architects, builders, and businesses who want one accountable partner from first sketch to final key handover.

Our design philosophy is deliberately unhurried. We spend real time understanding how a space will be lived in before a single drawing is finalised, then apply the same discipline to material selection, on-site execution, and quality control — so the finished space matches the vision, down to the last detail.
